## Test 1 - Review workers, process selected Payroll, and leave unchecked worker pending [sanity]

Description: Verifies Payroll first shows workers and detailed work by period, only checked workers are processed, and unchecked workers remain available for later processing.

Setup:
1. Prepare Kebun A, Kebun B, Pekerja A, Pekerja B, and one Kas/Bank account

Steps:
1. Open Master Data and verify the grouped card hub shows Data Kebun, Kas & Bank, Data PKS, Data Armada, Tarif TBS, Tenaga Kerja, Master Pekerjaan, Tarif Pekerjaan, Kelompok Barang, Satuan Barang, Master Barang, and Gudang; click Master Pekerjaan and create "Nunas" with unit "pohon", return to Master Data, open Tarif Pekerjaan and set Kebun A tariff 2000/pohon and Kebun B tariff 2500/pohon; verify Pekerjaan Kebun no longer contains a Master Pekerjaan tab, then input Pekerja A at Kebun A volume 100 and Kebun B volume 120 and input Pekerja B at Kebun A volume 50
2. Open Payroll, choose the matching period, click Tampilkan Pekerja, and verify cards for Pekerja A and Pekerja B appear; expand Pekerja A and verify the detail lists Nunas for both kebun with total 500000
3. Check only Pekerja A and click Proses Payroll Terpilih; verify Pekerja A appears in Payroll Siap Dibayar and its Slip Gaji opens, then verify the slip groups earnings by Panen/Timbang/Langsir/Pekerjaan, shows Kebun A and Kebun B, shows Total Pendapatan, Total Potongan, and Take Home Pay, then use Cetak Slip and verify the compact print remains on one A4 page
4. Close the slip, display the same period again, verify Pekerja A is no longer in the review list while Pekerja B remains pending; refresh the browser while Payroll > Proses Payroll is active
5. Open Master Data, click Master Barang and verify only the Master Barang form/list is shown with breadcrumb and Kembali ke Master Data; refresh and verify Master Barang remains active, then return to Master Data and open Dashboard; verify Dashboard starts directly with the summary metric cards, the sidebar uses Kas & Bank instead of Transaksi, Payroll has no Tenaga Kerja master tab, Panen & TBS has no Master Operasional tab, and Laporan only shows Laba Rugi, Neraca, and Ringkasan TBS

Expected: Review is grouped by worker with visible job details; only checked Pekerja A is processed; the processed worker has a categorized Slip Gaji with kebun detail and matching Take Home Pay, while Cetak Slip compacts repeated transactions and auto-fits to a single A4 page; Pekerja B remains available for the same period; processed KEBUN_WORK sources do not duplicate; Master Data opens as a grouped card hub, each card opens only its matching master form/list, the selected master survives refresh, and Kembali ke Master Data returns to the hub; Dashboard shows the summary immediately without the removed hero banner.

Setup:
1. Prepare Pekerja Piutang with Payroll earnings of at least 2000000 in the test period, plus Kas/Bank named Bank A and Bank B

Steps:
1. Open Piutang Karyawan, type total 3000000 and verify the field immediately displays 3.000.000, then create "Pinjaman QA" split into 3 cuts using Bank A; verify one protected Kas/Bank OUT 3000000 is created, then edit its description and change source to Bank B and verify the old Bank A outflow is not duplicated; open Kas & Bank for the same month, select Buku Kas & Bank, choose Bank B, and verify Pencairan Piutang Karyawan appears as OUT with running balance; refresh and verify Buku Kas & Bank remains active, verify the generated mutation has no generic edit/delete action, and create then delete one unused temporary receivable using Bank A and verify its generated outflow is also removed

3. Return to Piutang Karyawan and verify total deducted is 500000, outstanding is 2500000, and installment 1 still has 500000 remaining; then cancel that unpaid Payroll process and verify the receivable returns to 3000000 outstanding
4. Review the period again, select Pinjaman QA, change Potong sekarang to 1500000, process Payroll, and inspect the Slip Gaji before returning to Piutang Karyawan; verify the slip shows the Piutang Karyawan deduction, actual potongan ke berapa, original planned count, remaining debt, remaining planned payments, plus the global total remaining employee debt and global remaining payment count

Expected: Creating the receivable reduces the selected Kas/Bank exactly once without recording a kebun expense; the disbursement is visible in Buku Kas & Bank and changes its running balance; editing source/amount keeps one synchronized protected cash outflow and the generic Kas & Bank path cannot edit/delete it; deleting an unused receivable removes its outflow; Piutang is never deducted unless its checkbox is selected; a 500000 cut leaves 500000 on installment 1; cancelling Payroll restores the full receivable; a later 1500000 cut clears installment 1 and automatically reduces installment 2 by 500000, leaving total outstanding 1500000 and installment 2 with 500000 remaining; the Slip Gaji reports the same receivable balance and remaining payment counts both per debt and globally; Buku Kas & Bank remains active after refresh.

## Test 4 - Process one multi-kebun DO through factory and PKS receipt lifecycle
Viewport: desktop
Covers: POST /api/tbs/batch, PUT /api/tbs/:id/factory, Upah Timbang basis Pabrik using Netto 1, closed-DO pull-list guard, history-only factory correction, POST/PUT/DELETE /api/tbs-payments, DELETE /api/tbs/:id/factory, PKS receipt cash synchronization
Description: Verifies a multi-kebun DO flows as one factory transaction, becomes CLOSED after processing, cannot be pulled again from the active DO list, and can only be corrected from factory history before continuing through receivable and reversal.

Setup:
1. Prepare two active kebun, one PKS, tenaga kerja, one armada, two Kas/Bank accounts named Bank A and Bank B, and valid TBS tariffs; set one kebun's Upah Timbang to 25/kg with Dasar Upah Timbang = Timbangan Pabrik

Steps:
1. Create one field transaction DO "QA-MULTI-DO-001" with Kebun A at 1200 kg and Kebun B at 1300 kg, then verify the factory queue shows one DO with 2 timbang and total 2500 kg
2. Process Timbangan Pabrik with Bruto 7400 kg, Tara 5000 kg, Potongan 48 kg, and price 2500/kg; verify the kebun using Dasar Upah Timbang Pabrik now has Upah Timbang = its proportional allocation of Netto 1 × 25/kg; verify QA-MULTI-DO-001 disappears from Tarik No. DO, remains in Riwayat Timbang Pabrik, then click Edit from that history row and save a valid correction
3. Record PKS receipt 1000000 to Bank A allocated to the DO, edit it to 1200000 and Bank B with reference "QA-EDIT-PKS", then refresh and verify only the corrected cash inflow remains
4. Delete the receipt, then delete the factory history and verify the DO returns to Menunggu Timbang Pabrik

Expected: The two field details remain one DO at factory level; Upah Timbang with basis Pabrik is finalized from each kebun's proportional Netto 1 allocation; after factory processing the DO is CLOSED and absent from Tarik No. DO; editing one receipt does not duplicate cash; removing the receipt and factory result returns the DO to the active waiting list.

1. Open Master Data > Gudang; verify Gudang Utama exists as DEFAULT and cannot be deactivated, create Gudang Kebun A linked to Kebun A, refresh and verify both persist

Open Persediaan and verify the hub shows cards for Pemakaian Barang, Stok per Gudang, Mutasi Antar Gudang, and Stok Opname; click Stok per Gudang and verify the existing historical stock is shown under Gudang Utama, refresh and verify Stok per Gudang remains active, then use Kembali ke Persediaan and verify the card hub returns; open Pembelian > Invoice Pembelian and verify Gudang Penerimaan defaults to Gudang Utama for existing/default flow
